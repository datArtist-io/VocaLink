FAQ

Honest answers.

How many languages are really supported?

Translation covers 130+ languages via Google. Live speech recognition covers 80+. About 35 stream through Deepgram for best quality and speaker labels, and the rest use your browser's built-in recognizer. Any language can also be typed or pasted.

Is my audio stored?

No. Audio goes straight from your browser to Deepgram for transcription. VocaLink stores nothing. Your API key is kept only in your browser's local storage.

Why do I need a Deepgram key?

It authenticates the streaming connection. There is free credit on signup at console.deepgram.com. Without one, the browser engine still works for supported languages.

Why does it not work when I open the file directly?

Browsers restrict the microphone on file:// pages. Run python3 -m http.server 8000 in the folder and open localhost:8000.
