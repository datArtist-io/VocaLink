How it works

Four steps, none of them yours to manage.

Once VocaLink is in the channel, the whole loop runs on its own - nobody sets a language, nobody presses translate.

01

Someone starts talking

In whatever language they actually speak - no picker, no setting to configure first.

02

VocaLink identifies it

The language is detected automatically from the speech itself, sentence by sentence.

03

It's translated

Into whichever language the server has set as its target - English, Spanish, French, and seven more, with real-time switching between them.

04

You see or hear it

Captions post instantly; spoken dubbing is optional and only in a voice its owner enrolled themselves.

What it does

Built around how a live conversation actually moves.

10 languages, automatically

Nobody has to tell VocaLink what's being spoken. It listens and works it out, sentence by sentence, live-switching between English, Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Russian, Portuguese, Japanese, Italian, and Dutch - even mid-conversation.

Captions that keep up

Translated text lands in the channel within a couple seconds of someone pausing - fast enough to actually follow a live conversation, not read it back later.

Voices, only by consent

Anyone can enroll their own voice so their translated speech is spoken back in it - but only they can do that for themselves. Nobody else's voice is ever used without them opting in directly.

Already where you're talking

No separate app, no one downloading anything. VocaLink joins the voice channel you're already in.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Does VocaLink store what people say?

Audio is processed to produce a transcript and translation in the moment and isn't kept afterward. Enrolled voice samples used for cloned dubbing are stored only for the person who enrolled them, and can be deleted anytime.

How many languages does it support?

VocaLink currently auto-detects and live-switches between 10 languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Russian, Portuguese, Japanese, Italian, and Dutch. Expanding that toward 100+ is on the roadmap - this page will be updated as more languages go live, not before.

Do I need to be a server admin to add it?

Yes - adding any bot to a Discord server requires the "Manage Server" permission.

Can I hear translations in my own voice?

Only if you enroll your voice yourself with explicit consent. Nobody else can enroll your voice on your behalf, and nobody's voice is ever cloned without them doing it themselves.

What happens if the subscription ends?

The server drops back to the Free tier's limits - nothing is deleted, translation just goes back to the free monthly cap.

What permissions does VocaLink need?

Connect and Speak in voice channels, and permission to send messages in whichever text channel you choose for captions.
