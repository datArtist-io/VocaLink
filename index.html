FAQ

Honest answers.

How many languages are supported?

Translation covers 130+ languages via Google. Auto-detect works across 10 languages through Deepgram. Many other languages stream through Deepgram with a chosen source, and the rest use your browser's built-in recognizer where available. Any language can also be typed or pasted.

Is my audio stored?

VocaLink stores nothing. Audio goes from your browser to Deepgram for transcription, and text goes to Google for translation. Your API key is kept only in your browser's local storage.

Why do I need a Deepgram key?

It authenticates the streaming connection, and there is free credit on signup at console.deepgram.com. Without a key, the browser engine still works for languages your browser supports.

Why does the mic not work when I open the file directly?

Browsers restrict the microphone on file:// pages. Run python3 -m http.server 8000 in the folder and open http://localhost:8000.
